About Me

Teresa Bissoo is a visual artist whose work explores symbolic, imaginative, and spiritually inspired themes through painting and visual storytelling.

Her creative process is guided by exploration and intuition, allowing ideas and imagery to develop naturally as each piece evolves.

Drawing upon a background in healing disciplines and martial arts, her artwork often reflects themes of reflection, nature, and inner spiritual experience.

In addition to her visual art practice, Teresa has performed as a vocalist at public and private events.

Artwork, performances, and creative projects are presented under the name Tree Bee.

Career

Teresa Bissoo is a visual artist, vocalist, author, illustrator, and performer whose creative work has been shaped by formal artistic training, vocal mentorship, visual art study, publishing, broadcasting, and community arts experience.

Vocal Training & Pedagogy

  • Studied jazz singing under Alf Coward, a pioneering Canadian jazz pianist, recording artist, and longtime private studio educator.
  • Trained in jazz and classical vocal techniques under Katalin Puscas, an accomplished European classical musician and former resident artist at Romania's Cluj-Napoca National Opera and the Oradea Grand Philharmonic.
  • Developed contemporary singing and theatrical performance styles under Shannon Kingsbury, a Canadian singer-songwriter, harpist, vocal coach, and Sheridan College musical theatre alumnus.

Artistic Lineage & Mentorship

  • Studied acrylic painting techniques under Sam Paonessa, an award-winning Canadian Plein Air artist and former senior illustrator and art director for Hallmark Cards Canada.
  • Advanced classical figure drawing skills under Jørgen Lauritzen, a landscape artist and Sheridan College faculty instructor.
  • Received praise from James Picard, an internationally recognized fine artist, filmmaker, and humanitarian.

Career Highlights

  • Featured live performances on Rogers TV (2009).
  • Contributed backing vocals on a studio project with Juno Award-winning reggae artist Elaine "Lil'Bit" Shepherd (2012).
  • Live music performance and guest co-host appearances on 102.1 Sound FM (2012).
  • Featured as a guest vocalist and interview guest on Afternoon Drive with DJ Cool (Colin Johnson) on 100.3 Sound FM (now CKMS-FM Radio Waterloo 102.7 FM) in Kitchener-Waterloo.
  • Had the honor of performing for Jamaican-born Reggae Music Award winner and Living Legend Jimmy Reid (2013).
  • Manager of the Guelph Artisans Store (2013).
  • Presented music and visual art performances at The Outpost, a local arts incubator and grassroots venue, with related coverage appearing in the University of Waterloo's student newspaper, Imprint (2014).
  • Performed a blend of jazz and rock music alongside multi-instrumentalist Syd Ursa at various local venues (2014-2015), including a concert hosted by The Listener's Group in Kitchener.
  • Performed music at the ANAF Club in Guelph, Ontario (2016).
  • Performed and recorded music at The Rusty Cactus Studio in Guelph, Ontario (2016-2017).
  • Performed at a private house-concert event alongside jazz musician Tim Louis.
  • Contributed visual artwork to the music video Water by Juno Award nominee Alysha Brilla (2017).
  • Author and illustrator of Music Within All Things (2019).
  • Member of the Guelph Creative Arts Association (2020).
  • Author and illustrator of Teddy the Nutty Pup (2026).

Recognition

  • The late Kitchener singer-songwriter Paul MacLeod, a beloved gifted solo artist and former member of the Canadian rock band Skydiggers, praised Teresa's vocal abilities and offered to write original music for her, before his passing in 2016.
  • Juno Award-winning Canadian rock musician Matthew Good commended Teresa's vocal work, describing her voice as "very beautiful".
  • Juno Award-winning jazz vocalist Kellylee Evans after her concert met Teresa backstage, recognizing her vocal talent and requested a demo recording.
  • Toronto jazz vocalist Monica Chapman recognized Teresa's singing, vocal style, and sense of vocal composition.
