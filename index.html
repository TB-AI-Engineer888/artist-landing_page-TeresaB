About Me

Teresa Bissoo is a visual artist whose work explores symbolic and spiritually inspired themes through painting and visual storytelling.

Drawing upon a background in healing disciplines and martial arts, her paintings are rooted in personal spiritual experience, intuition, and the natural world.

A vocalist and performer as well as a visual artist, Teresa has appeared at public and private events throughout her career.

All artwork, performances, and creative projects are presented under the name Tree Bee.

Career

Teresa Bissoo is a multidisciplinary artist whose creative work has been shaped by formal artistic training, vocal mentorship, visual art study, publishing, broadcasting, and community arts experience.

Artistic Lineage & Mentorship

  • She attended Springbank Art School in her formative years. Her work in visual art and creative writing was recognized by teachers and peers throughout elementary and high school, receiving awards.
  • Studied acrylic painting techniques under Sam Paonessa, an award-winning Canadian Plein Air artist and former senior illustrator and art director for Hallmark Cards Canada.
  • Advanced in classical life drawing skills under Jørgen Lauritzen, a landscape artist and Sheridan College instructor, known for doing baron landscapes for movie production and commercial sets for TV.

Vocal Training & Pedagogy

  • Studied jazz singing under Alf Coward, a pioneering Canadian jazz pianist, recording artist, and longtime private studio educator.
  • Studied singing under Susan Cuthbert, a Toronto-based actor, singer, dancer, teacher, director, choreographer, and writer, whose credits include performing in Anne of Green Gables – The Musical at the Charlottetown Festival, Canada's longest running annual musical theatre production.
  • Trained in jazz and classical vocal techniques under Katalin Puscas, an accomplished European classical musician and former resident artist at Romania's Cluj-Napoca National Opera and the Oradea Grand Philharmonic.
  • Developed contemporary singing and theatrical performance styles under Shannon Kingsbury, a Canadian singer-songwriter, harpist, vocal coach, and Sheridan College musical theatre alumnus.

Career Highlights

Visual Art

  • Former Manager of the Guelph Artisans Store, where her visual artwork was featured.
  • Presented music and visual art performances at The Outpost, a local arts incubator and grassroots venue, with related coverage appearing in the University of Waterloo's student newspaper, Imprint.
  • Contributed visual artwork to the music video Water by Juno Award nominee Alysha Brilla.
  • Member of the Guelph Creative Arts Association (2020).

Music & Performance

  • Contributed backing vocals on a studio project with Juno Award-winning reggae artist Elaine "Lil'Bit" Shepherd.
  • Had the honor of performing for Jamaican-born Reggae Music Award winner and Living Legend Jimmy Reid.
  • Performed a blend of jazz and rock music alongside multi-instrumentalist Syd Ursa at various local venues, including a concert hosted by The Listener's Group in Kitchener.
  • Performed music at the ANAF Club in Guelph, Ontario.
  • Performed and recorded music at The Rusty Cactus Studio in Guelph, Ontario.
  • Performed alongside talented jazz musician Tim Louis at a house concert event.

Broadcasting & Radio

  • Experience with live performance on broadcast radio and TV.
  • Featured as a guest vocalist and interview guest on Afternoon Drive with DJ Cool (Colin Johnson) on 100.3 Sound FM (now CKMS-FM Radio Waterloo 102.7 FM) in Kitchener-Waterloo.
  • Performed on Rogers TV with guitarist Syd Ursa.

Publishing

  • Author and illustrator of Music Within All Things.
  • Author and illustrator of Teddy The Nutty Pup.

Digital Media

  • Creator and executive producer of SkyBeat PulseField, a YouTube music channel featuring original lyrics and AI-assisted music productions (ongoing).

Recognition

  • Sheridan College fine arts instructor Jørgen Lauritzen took note of Teresa's exceptional artistic ability, stating that she possesses the rare quality that defines a true artist.
  • Received praise from James Picard, former professor at OCAD (Toronto) and internationally recognized fine artist, filmmaker, and humanitarian.
  • The late Kitchener singer-songwriter Paul MacLeod, a gifted solo artist and former member of the Canadian rock band Skydiggers, praised Teresa's vocal abilities and offered to write original music for her before his passing in 2016.
  • Juno Award-winning Canadian rock musician Matthew Good commended Teresa's vocal work, describing her voice as "very beautiful".
  • Juno Award-winning jazz vocalist Kellylee Evans met Teresa backstage following a concert, commended her vocal talent, and requested a demo recording.
